# Data sources and acquisition

## Simulation and analysis outputs

`data/processed/` contains supplied simulation summaries and aggregate human/field analysis results used by `make_figures.py`. These are precomputed inputs, not production raw trajectories or saved neural-planner weights. Copies of aggregate human/field outputs are also stored in the corresponding subdirectories. Numerical values have not been changed during packaging.

## Human experiments

Source: Koster, R. et al. *Deep reinforcement learning can promote sustainable human behaviour in a common-pool resource problem*. Nature Communications 16, 2824 (2025).

Obtain the public per-round records through that article's Data availability section. Run `python code/human.py INPUT.csv.gz out_human`. The source columns expected by the script include mechanism names, launch and round identifiers, pool states, four offers and four return amounts. Included `human_condition_summary.csv`, `human_facts.csv`, `calibration_targets.csv` and `human_reciprocation.json` are aggregate results computed for this study. No participant-level or per-round records are distributed here.

## Field commons

Source: Baggio, J. A. et al. *Explaining success and failure in the commons: the configural nature of Ostrom's institutional design principles*. International Journal of the Commons 10, 417–439 (2016). Supplementary File 1 is referenced by the manuscript at https://osf.io/s7zre.

Obtain Supplementary File 1 from the original source and extract its text, preserving Tables A4–A7 and their `Complete`, `Missing = 0` and `Missing = 1` blocks. `code/field.py` parses these tables and uses simulated institutional class scores (`h4_class_sims.csv`, generated by the `h4` driver stage). Distributed field results and sensitivity tables are outputs of the present analyses.

Source supplement text, reconstructed case records (`baggio2016_cases_reconstructed.csv`), the transcription of published coding-agreement values (`coding_agreement_A2.csv`), and the row-level reconstruction audit (`reconstruction_audit_A4.csv`) are excluded while redistribution terms remain unverified. They remain in the local research materials. None is required by the supplied 12-figure plotting command.
